The wall nobody warns you about

Every AI app builder sells the same dream: prompt to product in 20 minutes. None of them mention what happens after. The store review is where AI-built apps die — and each rejection costs 2 to 7 days.

01

Your app is "just a website"

Guideline 4.2. A WebView pointed at a URL with no native capability is a web clip. This is rejection #1 for Lovable, Bolt, Replit and Cursor builds.

02

Apple tightened the screws

In March 2026 Apple blocked App Store updates for apps built with Replit and Vibecode under guideline 2.5.2. Coverage in Forbes, MacRumors, AppleInsider. Reviewers now know what an AI build looks like.

03

Nobody generated the boring parts

Privacy manifest, account deletion, encryption declaration, Data safety form, demo account, StoreKit instead of Stripe. No prompt produces these. Every one of them is a hard blocker.

04

Google has a calendar wall

Personal developer accounts created after 13 Nov 2023 need 12 real testers for 14 consecutive days before production. You cannot buy around it. You can only start the clock earlier.

05

Auth breaks inside the shell

Supabase, Clerk, Auth0 and Cognito sessions rely on http origins and cookies. A Capacitor export serves local assets. Result: infinite login loop, broken OAuth redirect, silently failing Sign in with Apple.

06

Wrappers cost money forever

Median is a subscription with tiered plugin upcharges. Despia is about $250 per app. Agency submission services run $500–2000 per app. The Kit is a one-time price and you own the code.

Questions

The ones that matter before you pay.

Will this guarantee my app gets approved?

No, and anyone who promises that is lying to you. App Review is done by humans and they have discretion. What the Kit does is remove the ~90% of rejections that are predictable and mechanical: missing native capability, missing demo account, missing privacy manifest, missing account deletion, wrong payment provider, wrong Data safety answers.

I built my app in Lovable / Bolt / Replit / Base44. Does it apply?

Yes, that is exactly who it is for. Lovable exports a Capacitor project, Bolt and Replit give you a web build you can wrap, and Base44/v0 output is a standard web app. The starter works with any web build you can produce: Next.js, Vite, React, Vue, plain HTML.

Do I need a Mac?

For the iOS build, yes — Xcode only runs on macOS. Options: a used Mac mini, a Mac in the cloud (MacStadium, AWS EC2 Mac, Scaleway), or a friend's Mac for 2 hours. Android has no such requirement. If you have no Mac at all, start with Google Play and ship iOS later.

Why not just pay Median or Despia to wrap it?

You can, and they are good products. Median is a subscription with tiered plugin upcharges and a managed service priced in the thousands. Despia is roughly $250 per app, one-time. Neither one fixes your privacy manifest, your Data safety form, your demo account or your account deletion endpoint — which is where most rejections actually come from. The Kit is cheaper than one Despia licence and it teaches your AI agent to do the compliance work.

I can't code. Is this usable for me?

Yes, with an AI agent. Every module ships a prompt you paste into Cursor, Claude Code, Windsurf or Lovable's agent, and the agent produces the files. The playbook is written in plain language. If you cannot run a terminal at all, the Starter tier plus the prompts still solves most rejections; the Pro starter code assumes you or your agent can run npm install.
